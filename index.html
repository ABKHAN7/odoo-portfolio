Common questions

What buyers ask before booking.

Do you work on both Odoo Community and Enterprise?+

Both, versions 14 through 19 — most recent work is on 17, 18, and 19. If a feature genuinely requires Enterprise licensing, I flag it before you commit, so it factors into the decision, not the invoice.

Will you fix a previous developer's mess?+

Frequently — it's one of my most common engagements. The Diagnostic Sprint exists for exactly this: I audit what's there, tell you what's salvageable, and quote the cleanup honestly, even when the honest answer costs me the bigger project.

How do you handle scope creep?+

Every engagement starts from a written scope document. Anything outside it becomes a written change order with its own estimate and timeline. You stay in control — there are no surprise invoices, by design.

Can you sign an NDA?+

Yes. I regularly sign mutual NDAs before discovery calls when client data or proprietary processes are involved. Send yours, or use mine.

Do you host, or only develop?+

Both. I deploy to Odoo.sh, your own VPS (Nginx + SSL hardened), or hybrid setups. The right choice usually comes down to data residency, budget, and what your team can maintain — I'll advise, not upsell.

What happens after the engagement ends?+

You own everything — source code, documentation, deployment configs. Build & Ship engagements include a 14-day hypercare window. After that, extend on a retainer or hand maintenance to your in-house team; there's no lock-in either way.
